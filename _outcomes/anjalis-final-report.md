# **Overview of Key Activities & Accomplishments**

Over the past few months, my work has primarily focused on learning and applying HPC infrastructure, particularly in the areas of resource allocation, management, and monitoring. My hands-on experience with HPC has directly contributed to the successful execution of several key projects within our department.

In addition to my technical focus, I have been consistently engaged in weekly sync sessions, where I stay updated on project progress and coordinate efforts across the team. These sessions have been crucial in ensuring that our projects run smoothly and that any challenges are addressed promptly. My role in these syncs has allowed me to provide valuable input and maintain the momentum of ongoing projects.

To further enhance my technical capabilities, I have participated in a range of training sessions, including "Kubernetes for Beginners," "Docker for Beginners," and "Advanced Docker & Kubernetes." These trainings, which combined both instructional and lab hours, have equipped me with a comprehensive understanding of these essential tools. Additionally, I completed further technical training through programs such as the CIML Summer Institute, COMPLEC Code migration, Jupyter Notebook Quickstart, and HPC Security Recordings. These experiences have significantly expanded my expertise, enabling me to contribute more effectively to complex and technically demanding projects.

While my involvement in strategic initiatives such as SCIPE-NAIRR meetings and the NAIRR Kick-off event has been part of my responsibilities, my primary contributions lie in the technical and operational aspects of our projects. I have also successfully integrated CIP training into my role, using it to enhance departmental processes, support faculty with development of quickstarts, and improve project management and execution.

Overall, these activities and accomplishments have not only deepened my technical skills and knowledge but have also played a critical role in the ongoing success and innovation in department. My focus on HPC and continuous process improvement has made a tangible impact on our projects and has positioned me to contribute even more effectively in the future.

# **Challenges**

Challenges that I faced during this reporting period included the initial difficulty with learning Nautilus, which was tricky and required a significant adjustment period. Navigating the complexities of HPC infrastructure also presented challenges, with a steep learning curve in resource allocation, management, and monitoring. This sometimes led to delays as I worked to optimize server performance.

Managing multiple projects, training sessions, and sync meetings posed significant time management challenges, making it difficult to address unexpected issues promptly. Although Kubernetes and Docker were already in use in my projects, setting them up and integrating them effectively was initially challenging for me, requiring additional effort to master their practical applications. Additionally, job scheduling concepts during training proved to be challenging for me, necessitating extra time and practice to fully understand.

Implementing CIP training into my role faced some resistance and required extra effort to align with existing practices. Moreover, some aspects of the COMPLECS training were initially challenging, but reviewing the recordings significantly improved my understanding and helped me overcome these difficulties.

Overall, balancing these various demands and navigating through the complexities of new tools and concepts required careful time management. Despite these obstacles, I worked diligently to address them and keep our projects on track.
