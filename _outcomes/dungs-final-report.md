# **Overview of Key Activities & Accomplishments**

I have actively participated in all scheduled training, especially with the in-person training at SDSC. The training covers a wide range, rich and in depth of topics such as parallel computing, HPC batch computing, deep learning with neural network, Jupyter notebooks, Sparks, Tensorflow, containers with Singularity, etc.. with hands-on practices. 

Besides, I also participated in other training such as ACCESS training workshops, other ones,  and self-training with Kubernetes platform where CSUSB software has been deployed.

I continue to further study about those topics, especially in data analysis, machine learning and HPC parallel processing. As a CS part-time lecturer, I am thinking about teaching a HPC related course. 

# **Efforts devoted to faculty and accomplishments.**

Mainly using NRP computing resources, CSUSB has several [active research projects](https://www.csusb.edu/faculty-center-for-excellence/idat/high-performance-computing/current-and-past-hpci-projects) that use HPC.   Following are HPC systems at CSUSB.  

Multiple HPC Jupyter hubs dedicated to faculty research, and students to study data science and machine learning. Those Jupyter hubs include many research software stacks such as R-Studio, PySpark, Tensorflow, etc. 

Dedicated HPC desktop that includes popular software such as VS code, Blender, and others per requests. Faculty and Students can remotely access those software with HPC computing power provided on-demand.   

Dedicated VASP system ( [Vienna Ab Simulation Package](https://www.vasp.at/) ) for Chemistry faculty and students doing research in material science. Papers have been published in conferences and [Science Direct journal](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925838822039901?utm_campaign=STMJ_AUTH_SERV_PUBLISHED&utm_medium=email&utm_acid=258805043&SIS_ID=&dgcid=STMJ_AUTH_SERV_PUBLISHED&CMX_ID=&utm_in=DM307246&utm_source=AC_#sec0100).

Dedicated Metashape system, an [i](https://wadielhudi.com/3d-modeling-at-wadi-el-hudi/)nteractive 3D model for large archaeological sites in southeastern Egypt, [Wadi el Hudi](https://wadielhudi.com/3d-modeling-at-wadi-el-hudi/) that has processed thousands of images. 

Providing reliable and high performing research systems on shared computing resources like NRP, a complex distributed HPC cluster is challenging. Faculty and students need reliable, stable systems besides a need of high-performing computing. 

CSUSB site is a regular ACCESS’s satellite HPC workshops. CSUSB has been awarded Innovation Mini-grant to build a 64-core HPC server with 5 x Nivida A5000 GPUs for HPC related courses. I am a campus champion. I am participating in contributing to a joined NSF grant proposal for a regional Cyber Infrastructure.

# **Challenges**

As a MSI, CSUSB is a teaching institution, it does not have investments in research computing infrastructure. Faculty are tasked with a high teaching load, and do not have many research grants. These and other barriers would  be a challenge to HPC program to excel in general. HPC program at CSUSB, however, has been a success. It is ranked as a R2. We are aware of these barriers to overcome.

So far, I do not face any serious technical request that I was not able to resolve. Skills and experiences are on-gong learning curve. I feel confident as long as there is an ambition of advancing research computing, and the mentors and scientists at sister SDSC, UCSD, NRP we have a chance to learn and build collaborations.
