# Overview of Key Activities & Accomplishments

I try to improve myself in supporting machine learning and scientific computing. For the former, the main events are the two AI Bootcamps by the Ohio Supercomputing Center and the Cyberinfrastructure-Enabled Machine Learning Summer Institute (CIML) at San Diego Supercomputing center (SDSC). For the latter, I focused on MPI, OpenMP, and OpenACC. For MPI, I followed the suggestion of my mentor Rick Wagner of https://mpitutorial.com/tutorials/. For OpenMP, in addition to the formation on the internet, I went over the Introduction to OpenMP by Tim Mattson on YouTube, https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLLX-Q6B8xqZ8n8bwjGdzBJ25X2utwnoEG&si=ZXIFT-WcdX60PDJ9. These were further strengthened by the HPC and Data Science workshop at SDSC.
