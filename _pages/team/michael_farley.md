# Michael Farley 
- Senior Personnel, TIDE Project 
- Chief Technology Research Officer 
- SDSU

Michael Farley is the Chief Technology Research Officer at San Diego State University, where he leads the Research & Cyberinfrastructure group. His work focuses on facilitating research and managing campus-wide networks, computing, and storage resources for research purposes. He is senior personnel on the NSF-funded Technology Infrastructure for Data Exploration (TIDE) project, OAC 2346701, which aims to expand access to computational resources across the California State University (CSU) system. With 23 years of experience in higher education IT, Michael has held both technical and managerial roles, overseeing various areas of information technology.
