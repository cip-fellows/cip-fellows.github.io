# **Overview of Key Activities & Accomplishments**

As a researcher with a background in structural bioinformatics, I successfully transitioned into this role by leveraging my expertise in parallel computing and data management. The CIP training grant enabled me to gain a deeper understanding of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and software development. With the guidance of a CIP mentor, I also established valuable connections with fellow researchers through in-person conferences.

Through this comprehensive training, I developed a range of skills, including creating materials, documentation, and presenting soft skills. Notably, I created an introductory [GitHub repository](https://github.com/hovo1990/CIP_Nextflow_on_HPC) showcasing the power of Nextflow on HPC, specifically highlighting its applications on SDSC Expanse supercomputer. Nextflow is a workflow management system designed for data-intensive scientific applications. With Nextflow, users can write and execute complex workflows in a simple and flexible manner, with automatic caching and distributing tasks via schedulers like SLURM. The examples I created included running simple tasks, as well as more advanced scenarios such as:

* Tasks using conda environments  
* Containerized tasks using Singularity  
* Bioinformatics and biophysics-related exercises, such as:  
* Blast searches  
* Simulations using GROMACS and Amber

These real-world examples provide context for the practical applications of Nextflow.

I also created documentation for training faculty, researchers, students, and interns on containers, JupyterHub, SLURM, and Nextflow for PI\`s (Prof. Ruben Abagyan) cluster . This resource formed the basis for a tutorial series that empowered researchers at my PI's lab, equipping them with valuable tools to advance their work.

I have been actively involved in the NAIIR initiative by participating in hackathons,providing support during the NAIRR pilot community event, and also provide leading office hours.  
I have also successfully completing the CyberAmbassadors training series. 

Additionally, I'm currently exploring ways to harness Nextflow on Kubernetes within the National Research Platform (NRP) environment, providing practical insights on optimizing and developing sophisticated pipelines.
