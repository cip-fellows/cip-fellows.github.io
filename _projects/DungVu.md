Dr. Dung Vu earned a Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of California, Riverside, with a focus on multi-core parallel computing. He is a part-time lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and serves as the Lead High Performance Computing Analyst. Dr. Vu joined CSUSB’s HPC initiative to help integrate the university with the Science DMZ. As a Campus Champion, he works closely with faculty to support research computing and deploy research software on the National Research Platform’s cluster, contributing to numerous publications
