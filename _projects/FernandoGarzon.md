Software engineer with extensive experience building user-facing web applications in research and academic environments. At UC San Diego’s Supercomputer Center, he leads frontend development for OSC-IS, a blockchain-integrated platform using Angular, NestJS, and AWS. He has designed component-based interfaces, implemented Cypress test suites, and helped cut deployment times by 90%. His work focuses on clean architecture, usability, and performance. Fernando also mentors high school students through UCSD’s REHS program.
