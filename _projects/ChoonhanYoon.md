I received my Ph.D. in Computer and Information Science from Syracuse University and currently serve as a Research Computing Scientist at the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) at UC San Diego. My work focuses on the development and support of collaborative cyberinfrastructure platforms and scientific gateway applications. I have extensive experience in Web and Grid development, Drupal module customization, high-performance computing integration, and scientific data management. I have worked across a range of technologies, including HPC systems, cloud computing environments, and science gateway frameworks.
 
My current projects include contributing to the One Science Place platform ( a Science Gateway Platform) by developing custom Drupal modules to enhance research data sharing, participating in the development of the QuakeWorx science gateway to support seismic simulations, and advancing the NEMAR project by improving data search and exploration tools for neuroelectromagnetic research. I am committed to advancing research computing by building scalable, reliable platforms that promote collaborative scientific discovery and innovation.
